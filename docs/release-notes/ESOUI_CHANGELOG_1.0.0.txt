1.0.0
- Initial public release.
- Tracks configured Dynamic Encounters in Stonefalls, Glenumbra, and Auridon.
- Shows the localized encounter name and step text supplied directly by ESO.
- Shows the known step position, current objective progress, and remembered participation state.
- Chest notifications only for explicit, tested per-encounter rules; includes a separate movable and customizable chest alert window.
- Per-encounter cooldown, spawn window, and overdue counter after a detected encounter end, with adjustable earliest/expected respawn times.
- Full localization for English, German, French, Spanish, Russian, and Simplified Chinese (German fully reviewed; the others are complete machine translations).
